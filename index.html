The Top 3 Culprits of Water Intrusion

The majority of water intrusion on single-ply roof systems originates from just three locations

1

Overlap Seams

Heat-welded seams. Seam failures are the #1 cause of roof leaks — incomplete welds, voids, or contaminants allow water to travel under the membrane.

2

Penetration Flashings

Movement, poor installation, and deterioration around pipe penetrations cause leaks as sealant breaks down and moisture travels under the membrane.

3

Perimeter & Terminations

Water enters at the edges, termination bars, and counterflashings as sealant deteriorates or termination height is improper.

PROVE: A Controlled, Repeatable Process

Prepare. Review. Observe. Verify. Evaluate. The field-to-underwriting path that keeps the score connected to evidence.

P

Prepare

Define the building, roof system, scope, timing, known history, and evidence plan.

R

Review

Review installation, inspection, maintenance, repair, construction, warranty, weather, and prior assessment records.

O

Observe

Inspect current conditions and document the eight physical-condition domains.

V

Verify

Confirm identities, dates, locations, completion, qualifications, photographs, and evidence links.

E

Evaluate

Apply severity, extent, rules, and version control to produce a standardized, date-specific result.
